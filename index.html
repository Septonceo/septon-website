06 / Questions leaders ask

Asked in every boardroom. Answered here.

Can't we just build this ourselves?

You can log; logging isn't evidence. By the time you've built tamper-evident sealing, policy mapping, oversight capture, obligation coverage and audit-grade exports, and kept them current with the EU AI Act, you've built Septon, slower. The trade is time and focus.

Is this just EU AI Act compliance?

Compliance is the floor. The same records that satisfy the nine obligations are what let you delegate more to AI, and what compound into institutional judgment. Nobody should buy a ledger twice: once for the regulator, once for the business.

We already have model monitoring.

Keep it. Monitoring watches models: drift, accuracy, bias. Septon records decisions: who decided, on what basis, with what oversight. When a decision is challenged, a dashboard of model metrics is not an answer.

Will this slow our decisions down?

The opposite. Capture happens at the point of action, not as an extra step. And decisions speed up overall, because a defensible decision is one you can delegate, downward and to AI.

What's the engagement model?

Thirty days to a working result: leadership interviews in week one, a workshop with your team in week two, and by day 30 one of your real decisions running end-to-end in your ledger. Then an ongoing partnership, if the result earns it.

Do our people have to change tools?

No. Decisions stay in the tools your people already use: Claude, Copilot, your existing workflows. Septon records them where they happen.
